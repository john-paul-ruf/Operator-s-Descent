THE CONSOLE
◈ The Single Console

Every action in Operator's Descent routes through one console pinned to the bottom of the screen.

No context menus. No floating panels. No map-tapping. The playfield is a readout, not a control surface.

The console has seven mutually exclusive modes:

CMBT
PARTY
GEAR
TECH

LOG

MOVE — Explore the floor, 8-way movement

CMBT — Issue combat commands during encounters

PARTY — Inspect party stats, HP, CHARGE, conditions

GEAR — Equip/unequip items, manage inventory

TECH — Cast protocols, manage CHARGE, overclock

LOOT — Open containers, take items

LOG — Review recent events, copy share link

◈ Tap a mode tab to switch. The console expands to a fixed height and dims the playfield behind it. Tap again to collapse.
◈ MOVE Mode

Move your party through the floor using the directional pad. Eight-way movement with a corner rule — you cannot move diagonally past a blocked corner.

Keyboard: Arrow keys, Numpad, or WASD

Touch: Tap directional buttons

Auto-stop: Movement halts when a hostile enters line of sight, a container or descent point is discovered, or the party takes damage.

Fog of war: Unvisited cells are dark. Visited cells dim. In-LOS cells are visible. Line of sight uses shadowcast.

◈ COMBAT Mode

When contact is made, the view zooms to an 8×16 grid at the contact point. The floor geometry becomes the arena.

Combat is turn-based with initiative order. Each character gets one move action + 2 AP per turn.

D
B
G

AP cost: Attack 1 AP · Cast protocol 1 AP · Item 1 AP · Overclock 1 AP · Retreat 1 AP

Free actions: Weapon swap (once/turn) · Class signature

Targeting: Tap a target, then confirm. Keyboard: Tab to cycle, Enter to confirm.

Range bands: Adjacent (1) · Short (1-4) · Medium (1-8) · Long (3-16) · Blast (AoE)

Cover: Determined by edge-crossing between attacker and target.

◈ PARTY · GEAR · TECH

PARTY: View each character's attributes, HP, CHARGE, conditions, class, calibration count, and corruption contribution.

GEAR: Equip and unequip weapons, armor, and off-hand items. CORRUPT items show a warning before equipping. Consumables are used from here (or in combat).

TECH: View your protocol deck. See CHARGE available and cost per protocol. Cast protocols (select target if required). Overclock for extra effect at double cost — with a corruption risk on failure.

⚠ CORRUPT items are strictly stronger but permanently raise the danger clock rate. The danger clock triggers hunts — ambush encounters where the party is surrounded.
◈ LOOT · LOG

LOOT: Activates when adjacent to or standing on a container. Shows contents with full item details — stats, rarity, affixes, CORRUPT status. Take items individually or all at once.

LOG: Scrolling log of recent events — combat rolls, discoveries, damage, deaths. Every d20 roll is recorded with the attribute used, modifier, target, and outcome.

Share link: In LOG mode, copy your full run state to a URL under 1500 characters. Paste it on any device to resume bit-for-bit.

◈ Status Strip & Settings

Status Strip (top of screen): Shows depth, seed, party sigils with HP bars, and danger clock value.

Accent color: Each floor's environment theme sets the screen's accent color. You read where you are from the color before reading a number.

Seed: The world seed determines every floor, every loot roll, every encounter. The same seed always yields the same dungeon.

Settings: Master mute, per-layer audio volume (5 sliders), glitch toggle, reduced-motion override, scanline/grain toggle. All persist in localStorage.

Depth is the score. There is no win condition. A run resolves on party wipe. The depth reached is the entire result. Every tenth floor is a threshold — guaranteed elite, vault, and new theme.
